# Privacy Policy (template - have it reviewed by a lawyer)

**Merge Legion** ("the game") is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## What we collect
* Gameplay progress and settings (stored on your device and, if online, in Unity Gaming Services cloud save).
* An anonymous player ID, device identifiers (advertising ID where you allow it), approximate region (from IP), device model/OS, crash and performance data.
* Gameplay events and purchases (analytics and attribution).

## Why
To run the game and sync progress, show ads, process purchases, prevent fraud, measure and improve the game.

## Who receives it
Unity (Gaming Services), Google (Firebase, AdMob), AppLovin and its mediated ad networks, GameAnalytics, AppsFlyer, Apple / Google (purchases).
Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

## Your choices
* Change ad personalisation in **Settings > Privacy choices**. On iOS you can deny tracking.
* **Delete your data** in **Settings > Delete account and data**, or email [support address].
* EEA/UK/California residents can request access, correction or deletion at [support address].

## Retention
Cloud saves are kept until you delete them or the account is inactive for 24 months.
